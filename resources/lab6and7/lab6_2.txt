CYLIND,100,200,0,20,0,90

! 3. Mesh (Brick elements using sweep)
ESIZE,10
VSWEEP,ALL
*GET,NN,NODE,0,COUNT

! 4. Symmetry Boundary Conditions on cut faces (UX=0 on X=0 plane, UY=0 on Y=0 plane)
ASEL,S,LOC,Y,0

/SOLU
ANTYPE,STATIC

ASEL,S,LOC,X,100

! Load Step 3: Both pressures ramped to 0 (Time = 3)

ALLSEL
