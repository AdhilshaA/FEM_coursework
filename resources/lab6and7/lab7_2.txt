### Analytical Comparison Reference ($\delta_{\text{beam}}$)

To fill out your lab report table for Part 2(d), compare your FEA tip deflections ($w_{\text{FE}}$) against the classical Euler-Bernoulli beam formula[cite: 13]:

$$\delta_{\text{beam}} = \frac{P L^3}{3 E I}$$

Where:
* $P = 10\text{ N}$
* $L = 200\text{ mm}$
* $E = 200,000\text{ MPa}$
* $I = \frac{b t^3}{12} = \frac{20 \times 5^3}{12} \approx 208.33\text{ mm}^4$

$$\delta_{\text{beam}} = \frac{10 \times (200)^3}{3 \times 200,000 \times 208.33} \approx 0.384\text{ mm}$$
